Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

What the app does

"Road Rules Publisher" is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Road Rules Quiz Daily" Facebook Page. It publishes the owner's own images and videos to that Page and reads the Page's own performance statistics (for example views and follower counts).

Data we use

The app receives an access token for the Page owner's account and the Page identifier. It does not collect, store or process personal data of Page visitors, followers or any other Facebook users.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own systems. They are never published, sold or shared with third parties. Data is only sent to Facebook (Meta) to publish content and read Page statistics.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the app at any time in Facebook Settings under Business Integrations. After removal the token stops working and is deleted.

Contact

Send a message to the "Road Rules Quiz Daily" Page on Facebook.
