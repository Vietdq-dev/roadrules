User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

"Road Rules Publisher" does not collect or store personal data of Page visitors, followers or other Facebook users. The only data it holds is the Page owner's access token.

To remove the app and its data

  1. In Facebook, open Settings, then Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  2. Find "Road Rules Publisher" and choose Remove. The access token is revoked and deleted.

To request deletion of any data

Send a message to the "Road Rules Quiz Daily" Page on Facebook with the subject "Data deletion". We will confirm within 7 days that no data about you is held, or delete it if it is.
